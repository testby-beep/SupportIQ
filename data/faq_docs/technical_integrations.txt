CloudDesk Technical & Integrations FAQ

Q: Which third-party tools does CloudDesk integrate with?
A: CloudDesk natively integrates with Slack, Google Drive, GitHub, Jira, and Zapier.
Pro and Enterprise plans also get access to our public REST API and webhook system for
custom integrations. Free plan users can use Zapier only, with a limit of 100 tasks/month.

Q: Where can I find API documentation?
A: Full API docs are available at docs.clouddesk.example/api, including authentication
(API keys, OAuth2 for Enterprise), rate limits, and example requests in Python, JavaScript,
and cURL. Rate limits are 60 requests/minute on Pro and 600 requests/minute on Enterprise.

Q: Why is my Slack integration not sending notifications?
A: This is usually caused by one of three things: (1) the Slack app permissions were
revoked, (2) the connected Slack channel was deleted or archived, or (3) notification
settings were turned off under Settings > Integrations > Slack > Notification Preferences.
Reconnecting the integration from scratch resolves most cases.

Q: Does CloudDesk have a mobile app?
A: Yes, CloudDesk is available on iOS (14+) and Android (10+). The mobile app supports
viewing projects, commenting, and notifications, but bulk editing and admin settings are
currently desktop-only.

Q: Can I export my data?
A: Yes. Under Settings > Data > Export, you can export all projects as CSV, JSON, or PDF.
Enterprise customers can also request a full account data export via the API for
migration purposes.

Q: What browsers are supported?
A: We officially support the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
Internet Explorer is not supported. If you experience rendering issues, clearing your
browser cache or trying an incognito window usually resolves it.

Q: Is there a status page for outages?
A: Yes, status.clouddesk.example shows real-time uptime and incident history. You can
subscribe to email or SMS alerts for outages affecting your region.

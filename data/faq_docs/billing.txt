CloudDesk Billing & Subscriptions FAQ

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: CloudDesk accepts all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal,
and for annual Enterprise plans, we support bank transfer / wire payment. We do not
currently accept cryptocurrency.

Q: How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Change Plan. Upgrades take effect immediately and you'll be
charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the billing cycle. Downgrades take effect
at the start of the next billing cycle, so you keep your current plan's features until then.

Q: Do you offer refunds?
A: We offer a full refund within 14 days of your first payment if you're on a monthly plan
and haven't used more than 20% of your included usage quota. Annual plans are refundable
within 30 days under the same usage condition. After these windows, we do not issue refunds
for partial billing periods, but you can cancel anytime to stop future charges.

Q: My card was declined, what should I do?
A: First, confirm your card hasn't expired and has sufficient funds. Then check Settings >
Billing > Payment Method to make sure the billing address matches your bank's records.
If the issue persists, try a different card or contact your bank, since some banks block
recurring international charges by default.

Q: How does the free trial work?
A: All new accounts get a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan with no credit card required.
At the end of the trial, your account automatically moves to the Free plan unless you add
a payment method. You will not be charged automatically.

Q: Can I get an invoice for my company's accounting team?
A: Yes. Every payment automatically generates a PDF invoice available under Settings >
Billing > Invoice History. For custom invoice details (PO numbers, company address changes),
Enterprise customers can contact billing@clouddesk.example to request a custom template.

Q: What happens if my payment fails on renewal?
A: We retry the payment 3 times over 7 days. During this period your account stays active
in a "payment overdue" state. If all retries fail, your account is downgraded to the Free
plan and you'll lose access to Pro features (advanced integrations, priority support,
unlimited projects) until payment is resolved.

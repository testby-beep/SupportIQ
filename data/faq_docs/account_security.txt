CloudDesk Account & Security FAQ

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your registered email. You'll
receive a reset link valid for 60 minutes. If you don't receive the email within a few
minutes, check your spam folder or contact support since your email may not be verified.

Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication (2FA)?
A: Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication and choose either an authenticator
app (Google Authenticator, Authy) or SMS-based codes. We strongly recommend the
authenticator app method since SMS can be intercepted via SIM-swap attacks.

Q: Can I change the email address associated with my account?
A: Yes, under Settings > Account > Email. You'll need to verify the new email address
before the change takes effect, and you'll receive a confirmation at your old email as
a security measure. If someone else changed your email without permission, contact
support immediately at security@clouddesk.example.

Q: How do I delete my account permanently?
A: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This is irreversible after a 30-day grace
period, during which you can log back in to cancel the deletion. All your projects, data,
and billing history are permanently erased after the grace period ends.

Q: Is my data encrypted?
A: Yes. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ and at rest using AES-256.
Enterprise plans additionally support customer-managed encryption keys (CMEK) for
organizations with strict compliance requirements.

Q: I think my account was compromised. What do I do?
A: Immediately change your password, enable 2FA if you haven't already, and review
Settings > Security > Active Sessions to revoke any sessions you don't recognize. Then
contact security@clouddesk.example so our team can review account activity logs.

Q: How many team members can I add to my workspace?
A: Free plan: up to 3 members. Pro plan: up to 20 members. Enterprise: unlimited members
with role-based access control (Admin, Editor, Viewer, and custom roles).
